Poor prognostic factors

According to the American College of Gastroenterology, the following factors are associated with an increased risk of hospitalisation, colectomy or poor disease outcomes.

1Age younger than 40 years at diagnosis
2Extensive colitis
3Severe endoscopic disease
4Previous hospitalisation for colitis
5Elevated C-reactive protein
6Low serum albumin

Treatment selection

Treatment selection should be guided by both current disease activity and prognosis.

Patients with mild symptoms but high-risk features, such as corticosteroid dependence or previous hospitalisation, may require therapies appropriate for moderate-to-severe ulcerative colitis.⁠​‌​​​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌​​​‌​​​​​​‌​​‌​​​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌​​‌​​​‌‌​‌​​‌​​‌​​​​​​‌​​​​​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​​​​​​‌​‌​​‌‌​‌‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​​‌​​​‌​​​​​​‌‌‌‌‌​​​​‌​​​​​​​‌‌​​‌​​​‌‌​​​​​​‌‌​​‌​​​‌‌​‌‌​⁠
